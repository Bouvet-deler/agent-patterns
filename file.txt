Oh Bouvet Island, so far away,
Lost in the sea, where wild winds play.
Cloaked in mist and icy white,
You shimmer in the polar night.

No trees to whisper, no birds to sing,
Just howling gales and penguin wings.
A land untouched, remote and cold,
With stories waiting to be told.

Seals rest upon your frozen ground,
Waves crash and echo, a haunting sound.
Your cliffs stand proud against the storm,
In your solitude, a beauty is born.

Few have set foot on your lonely shore,
Yet in dreams, you’re so much more.
A mystery wrapped in snow and sea,
Bouvet Island, wild and free.

On maps you’re just a tiny mark,
But in the heart, you leave a spark.
Here’s to you, forgotten land,
A wonder shaped by nature’s hand.
